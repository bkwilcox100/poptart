## Settings, reorganized

Settings are now grouped by what they do instead of by which project they
came from, and there's a search box that spans all of them — type "paste" or
"hotkey" and the setting comes to you, wherever it lives. Snippets sit
alongside custom words, and AI cleanup is a normal setting rather than
something hidden behind an "experimental" toggle.

## A real Pop-Tart in the menu bar

The tray icon is no longer Handy's toaster.

## Fewer stray question marks

Ending a dictation with a spoken punctuation command ("...is that final,
question mark") no longer adds a period on top of it, which used to leave AI
cleanup guessing and produce things like `"Final".?`
